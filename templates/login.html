1. Traditional Railway (The Problem)
  • ❌
    Blind Timetables: ETAs rely on fixed paper schedules. When rain or red signals happen, arrival times are wrong by hours.
  • ❌
    Platform Rush Chaos: Platforms are announced 2-5 minutes before docking, causing stampedes with heavy luggage.
  • ❌
    Chain-Reaction Delays: If 1 train stops, 4 trains behind get stuck on main track with no automatic diversion.
  • ❌
    Human Safety Dependency: Relies only on visual driver signals; blind curves and dense fog cause fatal accidents.
2. What Star Coders Added (How We Solved It)
  • ✅
    Physics-Driven Live ETA: Fuses GPS speed (158 km/h), rain grip, and ahead red lights to give real-to-the-second ETAs.
  • ✅
    Smart 20-Min Platform Allocator: Dynamically reserves and displays the exact platform 20 min before arrival.
  • ✅
    Autonomous Bypass Loop Routing: AI automatically diverts slow trains to loop lines so express trains never stop.
  • ✅
    Kavach 4.0 Auto-Stopping: Enforces a 1,200m electronic safety bubble that auto-brakes the train if danger appears.
