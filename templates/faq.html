Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about IRCTC RailAI™, real-time train radar, dynamic physics ETAs, Kavach 4.0 collision prevention, and platform allocations.

Real-Time Tracking & Dynamic ETA

How does RailAI dynamic ETA differ from standard NTES schedule timetables? ETA ENGINE

Traditional systems rely heavily on static timetables and fixed station-to-station estimates. When rain, track maintenance, or red signals occur, static arrival times can be inaccurate by hours.

RailAI continuously streams real-time GPS telemetry, live train velocity (up to 158.5 km/h), ahead signal aspects, and weather telemetry to recalculate a physics-driven ETA updated every second.

Do passengers need to register or enter a password to use Live Radar? 100% FREE

No login or password is required for passengers. The Passenger Live Radar is completely free and publicly accessible. Simply select your train (e.g. 15558 Amrit Bharat or 99001 Hydrogen Express) and station to see live speed, dynamic ETA, and assigned platform immediately.

Smart Platform Allocation & Bay Management

How does the system prevent station platform stampedes? 20-MIN ADVANCE

In conventional operations, platforms are often assigned only 2 to 5 minutes before docking, leading to intense passenger rushes and bottleneck stampedes on foot overbridges.

The Smart Platform Allocator analyzes 8-platform bay readiness, turnaround countdowns, and track interlocking in advance, allocating and locking in the exact platform 20 minutes prior to arrival.

Can Station Masters manually override platform or signal assignments? INTERLOCKING

Yes. Through the Station Master Control Room, station masters can view real-time platform occupancy and turn-around countdowns, and execute 1-click signal overrides connected directly to the interlocking database with automatic safety fail-safes.

Kavach 4.0 Collision Prevention & Weather Safety

What is the Kavach 4.0 electronic safety bubble? 1,200m BUBBLE

Kavach 4.0 is an indigenously developed Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system. RailAI integrates continuous trackside RFID tags, wayside radio transponders, and onboard computers to maintain a dynamic 1,200-meter electronic safety bubble around the locomotive. If an obstruction or headway violation occurs, the system initiates autonomous emergency braking in under 15 milliseconds.

How does RailAI respond to heavy rainfall, fog, or slippery tracks? WEATHER AI

The Weather & Track Telemetry agent monitors ambient precipitation and wheel-rail adhesion. When wet or degraded adhesion is detected, the AI automatically caps maximum operating velocity from 158 km/h down to a safe 110 km/h and extends braking distance profiles to prevent wheel slip.
