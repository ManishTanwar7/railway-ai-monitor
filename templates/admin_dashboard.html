Mathematical & Neural Calculation Formulations

1. Train ETA Dynamic Integration

ETA Forecaster
ETA = ∑ [ Δd_segment / ( v_target · (1 - C_congestion) · (1 - W_weather) ) ] + Dwell_Time

Integrates along track profiles. Each segment accounts for mechanical acceleration, curvature friction, ahead yellow signal aspects, and weather traction coefficients.

2. Delay Propagation Regression

Delay Predictor
Predicted_Delay = α · Dwell_Anomaly + β · P(Junction_Bottleneck) + γ · Weather_Penalty

Evaluates cascade delays across intersecting routes. Anticipates junction queuing and triggers automated loop siding bypasses.

3. Queuing Congestion & Bottleneck Index

Congestion AI
ρ_corridor = λ_inbound / (μ_service · c_tracks) + Spillback_Factor(Δt)

Uses M/M/c queuing theory. When track occupancy exceeds 0.80, junctions are marked critical and outer loop diversions are recommended.

4. Atmospheric Friction & Rail Buckling Risk

Weather Analyzer
W_impact = α · Rain^0.55 + β · max(0, Wind - 45) + γ · (ΔT_rail)^1.8

Calculates rail-wheel adhesion degradation (μ drops from 0.38 dry down to 0.18 wet) and thermal stress to safeguard high-speed trains.
